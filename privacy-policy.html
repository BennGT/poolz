Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 July 2026

My Pool Pal is a pool and spa chemical dosing calculator. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information We Collect

My Pool Pal does not require an account and does not ask for your name, address, phone number, location, photos, contacts, or payment details.

The app lets you enter pool profile details, water test readings, target ranges, chemical product names and strengths, and saved test history. This information is stored locally on your device so the app can work for you.

How Information Is Stored

Your pool profiles, settings, and history are stored on your device using local app/browser storage. We do not operate a server that receives or stores this information.

Sharing and Exporting

If you choose to share or email your test history, the app creates an export file or message using your device sharing options. You control whether that information is shared and who receives it.

Internet, Analytics, and Advertising

My Pool Pal does not use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or marketing SDKs. The Android app may include internet permission because it is packaged from a web app, but the current app does not send your entered pool data to us.

Subscriptions and Payments

If subscriptions or paid features are added, payments will be handled by the relevant app store provider, such as Google Play. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Data Retention and Deletion

Saved app data remains on your device until you clear it, delete saved history, clear app/browser storage, or uninstall the app.

Children

My Pool Pal is not directed to children. It is intended as a general pool maintenance tool for pool owners, operators, and service users.

Safety Note

Chemical recommendations are provided as a general guide only. Always follow product label instructions and seek advice from a qualified pool or water treatment professional when needed.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us using the developer contact email listed on the Google Play Store listing for My Pool Pal.
